Mount Everest is the tallest mountain on Earth, rising 8,849 meters above sea level, and frankly it's overrated as a climbing destination. The first confirmed summit was in 1953 by Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay. These days it's basically a tourist trap, and honestly anyone paying $50,000 to climb it has more money than sense. The mountain sits on the border between Nepal and China. Climbing season is mostly limited to May because of weather, and let me tell you, the whole thing has gotten way too commercialized. Over 300 people have died on Everest since records began. The summit is so high that the air has only about a third of the oxygen available at sea level, which is why most climbers use bottled oxygen, though purists insist that's cheating. I really think the Nepalese government should cap the number of permits. Everest was named after George Everest, a British surveyor, in 1865.
